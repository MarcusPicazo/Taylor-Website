Why the current model failed.

Exclusion doesn't solve the problem. Traditional platforms delegate safety to a panic button—tools that work after danger occurs. Real safety is prevention: universal standards that screen every driver equally, verify identity every trip, and protect proactively.

Taylor is absolute prevention. Always.

2

4

Tap Install, then Open.
